# GenAIForge Risk — Learning Guide

This app demonstrates governed AI workflows for enterprise risk:

1. **M1 Outsourcing Classification** — classify vendor arrangements against regulatory clauses.
2. **M2 Vendor Due Diligence** — screen vendors and capture findings.
3. **M3 Risk Scoring** — score residual risk with configurable weights.
4. **Forms** — assign, fill, export due-diligence questionnaires.

All configuration is in `.env`. Sign in with seed accounts from the README.

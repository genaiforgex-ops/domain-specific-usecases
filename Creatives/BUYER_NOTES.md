## Buyer pain mapped

1. **Brand governance at scale** — hundreds of variants (Nike-style) without breaking lockups or claims.
2. **Agency cost & cycle time** — event creative (Apple-style) needs days/hours, not 11-day cycles.
3. **HITL approval** — Legal hard-stop on disclosures and banned claims; RACI on publish.
4. **Performance attribution** — messy post-Sandbox world; studio closes learning loop with CTR + cited market context.
5. **Retail media tax** — format sprawl across networks without multiplying agency packs.

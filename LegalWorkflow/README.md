## 2-minute client pitch

1. **Dashboard** — Hero “why buy” (HITL · citations · 7yr audit · obligations). Tiles deep-link to Matters, Radar, Obligations, MSA, etc. Daily brief actions. **CLM buyer research pulse** cites 2026 RFP themes (playbooks, citations, obligations, SOC2).
2. **LegalBot** — Modes Review/Research/Draft; streaming answers with **source citations** (playbook §). Extra prompts for SOC2 language and obligation summaries.
3. **MSA Automation** — CloudNest tracker: stage timeline, AI review with HITL note, **negotiation memory** (3-month cap rejection history). Advance stage.
4. **Playbooks** — Standard vs deviation table (escalate/reject/review). Jump to LegalBot.
5. **Radar** — Regulatory items **plus** buyer RFP themes (playbook AI, obligations, trust) with illustrative citations → update playbook.
6. **Templates + Comparison** — Approved library (incl. AI subprocessor addendum); NovaPay hunk navigator with material flags.
7. **Obligations + Matters** — Richer post-signature tracker (SOC2, escrow, renewals); intake queue with SLAs (incl. inference-only advice).
8. **Trust center** — SOC2/ISO (demo-labeled), inference-only, AI audit log, residency, CSV export, Pilot/Production packages.
9. **Tasks** — Priority filters, claim/complete (existing).
